Features

Built for boards that never stay small

Every column gets its own color, its own tags, its own rules — whether AI built it in seconds or you laid it out card by card.

AI generation

Describe it, watch it stream in

Type a project in plain language and boards, columns, cards, and tags generate live — built to handle complex, multi-disciplinary setups, not just to-do lists.

Local-first

Nothing leaves your browser

Boards, cards, tags, and images live in IndexedDB on your machine. No accounts, no database, no server holding your data.

Today's board

A board that resets itself

Drop in your appointments and priorities and get a daily planning board with an hours-left badge — it auto-expires after 24 hours so today never clutters tomorrow.

Tags & filters

Find anything in one search

Tag cards by project, mood, or person, then filter across every board at once — search and it doesn't matter which column it's hiding in.

Drag & drop

Reorder without friction

Smooth, touch-enabled drag-and-drop across cards and columns, so rearranging priorities feels as fast as thinking of them.

Backup & restore

Your data, exportable anytime

Export every board and setting to a single file, or import an old backup — with automatic cleanup so nothing comes in broken.

Privacy

Your boards stay on your machine

No server database, no accounts, no telemetry. AI requests go straight from your browser to your chosen model provider — nowhere else.

Storage

IndexedDB, not the cloud

Boards, cards, tags, and attached images are stored locally in your browser — nothing syncs to a server by default.

API keys

Kept local, sent once

Your AI key stays in your browser's storage and is never routed through or held on a third-party server.

No tracking

Nothing watching you use it

No telemetry, no cookie tracking, no analytics running in the background.
